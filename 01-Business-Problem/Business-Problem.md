# Business Problem

## Background

A retail bank currently offers personal loans through a
digital application process.

Customers can submit loan applications online, but several
activities still require manual intervention.

## Current Challenges

The bank has identified the following challenges:

1. Long loan approval times
2. Manual document verification
3. Duplicate data entry
4. Delays in credit assessment
5. High number of applications requiring manual review
6. Limited visibility of application status
7. Customer application abandonment

## Business Impact

These problems can result in:

- Increased operational costs
- Poor customer experience
- Longer turnaround time
- Increased workload for loan officers
- Lost potential customers

## Business Objective

The bank wants to reduce the average loan approval time
while maintaining appropriate risk and compliance controls.

## Project Goals

The project aims to:

- Understand the current process
- Identify bottlenecks
- Define business requirements
- Improve automation
- Reduce manual processing
- Improve customer visibility
- Define measurable KPIs

## Success Metrics

The proposed solution will be evaluated using:

- Average loan approval time
- Percentage of automated applications
- Manual review percentage
- Application abandonment rate
- First-time document verification rate
