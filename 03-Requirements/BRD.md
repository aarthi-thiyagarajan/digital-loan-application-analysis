# Business Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the business
requirements for improving the digital personal loan
application process.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Reduce loan approval time
- Reduce manual processing
- Improve customer experience
- Improve application tracking

## 3. Scope

### In Scope

- Loan application
- Document upload
- KYC verification
- Credit assessment
- Risk assessment
- Loan decision
- Customer notification

### Out of Scope

- Loan pricing
- Marketing campaigns
- Collection processes
- Existing loan servicing

## 4. Stakeholders

- Customers
- Loan Officers
- Credit Analysts
- Compliance
- Product Team
- IT Team

## 5. Business Requirements

BR-001: Customers must be able to submit loan applications
online.

BR-002: Customers must be able to upload required documents.

BR-003: The system must validate mandatory documents.

BR-004: The system should automatically perform eligibility
checks.

BR-005: High-risk applications must be routed for manual review.

BR-006: Customers must be able to track application status.

BR-007: Customers must receive notifications when the status
changes.

## 6. Success Criteria

The solution should reduce processing time and manual
intervention while maintaining compliance requirements.
